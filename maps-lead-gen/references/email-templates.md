# 3-sentence local-fact emails for 3xAgentic (drafts only - John sends)

Shape: [one TRUE local fact] [one offer] [one question with a free, useful offer]. Phone-readable. No "saw your
Google listing". Fill from the lead's columns only; if a column is empty, pick a template that doesn't need it.
Footer on every email (CAN-SPAM): John's postal address + `Not the right person, or not interested? Reply "no" and I won't email again.`

Greeting: `Hi Dr. {owner_last},` / `Hi {owner_first},` only when owner_first is a real found name (not "not found")
and the email is the owner's own or a general inbox. Otherwise `Hi there,`.

## Facts you may use (from local_facts.py)
- competitor_reviews: `Noticed {competitor}, about {mi} mi from you, is sitting on {n} Google reviews.`
- own_rating (4.0+ only): `{business} has a {rating}-star rating from {n} Google reviews, which is a lot of happy patients.`
- density: `I counted at least {n} other {niche_plural} within {r} miles of {business} on the map.`
- nearest: `Noticed {competitor} is about {mi} mi from {business} on {street}.`

## Offers (pick ONE per campaign)
- Missed-call text-back: `We set up missed-call text-back for {niche_plural}, so every call you can't pick up gets a friendly text within seconds and the {customer} books with you instead of calling the next office.`
- AI receptionist: `We set up an AI receptionist for {niche_plural} that answers every call 24/7, books the appointment and handles the usual questions, so nothing goes to voicemail.`
- Speed-to-lead: `We set up instant replies for {niche_plural}, so every web form or booking request gets a personal text back within 60 seconds while the {customer} is still deciding.`

## Questions with a free useful offer
- `Want me to send over a free 1-page comparison of how {business} ranks on Google Maps against {competitor or "the offices around you"}?`
- `Want a free audit of your Google reviews that shows which ones mention calls, wait times or follow-up?`
- `Want me to call your office after hours tonight and send you a 1-minute recording of what a new {customer} hears?` (only if John will really do it)

## Example (built from a real lead in the Austin test, address is a placeholder)
Subject: 8+ dental offices within 2 miles

Hi Dr. Chang,

I counted at least 8 other dental offices within 2 miles of Daybreak Dental on the map. We set up instant replies for dental offices, so every web form or booking request gets a personal text back within 60 seconds while the patient is still deciding. Want me to send over a free 1-page comparison of how Daybreak Dental ranks on Google Maps against the offices around you?

John
3xAgentic

## Follow-ups (same thread, short)
- Day 3: `Hi {name}, just bumping this. Happy to send the comparison either way, no call needed.`
- Day 7: `Last one from me: if missed calls aren't a problem at {business}, reply "all good" and I'll close this out.`
Stop after 2 follow-ups. Anyone who replies "no" or "stop" goes on the suppression list the same day.

## Don'ts
- No invented numbers ("you're losing $10k/month"), no fake familiarity, no stated results 3xAgentic can't show.
- No ratings under 4.0, no negative review quotes in the email.
- No crypto/blockchain/Web3 words. 3xAgentic is AI automation only.
- Never send from the agent. Drafts go to John.
