# 3xAgentic pricing guide (USD)

| Tier | Build (one-time) | Monthly (support, hosting, tweaks) | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pilot** | $500–750 | none | One workflow, built and running in ~2 weeks. Proves value before a bigger commitment. |
| **Starter** | $1,000–2,000 | $250–400/mo | 1–2 core automations (e.g. missed-call text-back + lead follow-up), basic reporting. |
| **Growth** | $2,500–4,000 | $500–800/mo | Several connected workflows (AI receptionist, booking, CRM sync, review requests), monthly optimisation. |
| **Pro** | $5,000–10,000 | scoped per client | Custom multi-system build, integrations, dashboards, priority support. |

## How to pick a number inside a range

- Start low in the range when: one simple tool stack, the client is small (1 location, < 10 staff), the workflow is a known template.
- Go high when: multiple locations, messy or legacy software, compliance needs (HIPAA-adjacent clinics), custom integrations, tight deadline.
- Monthly fee covers: hosting/API usage pass-through up to a stated cap, monitoring, small changes, a monthly check-in. Say what is *not* included.
- Always offer the Pilot as the easy first step. Credit the Pilot fee toward Starter/Growth if they upgrade within 30 days (only if John has said this is OK for the client).

## Rules

- Quote in USD. Payment terms default: 50% to start, 50% on delivery for builds; monthly billed in advance.
- Never promise specific revenue or ROI numbers. Talk in mechanisms ("every missed call gets a text in under a minute") and the client's own numbers if they shared them.
- Third-party costs (phone numbers, AI usage, software seats) are listed separately and paid by the client or passed through at cost.
- John has the final say on every quote. Present pricing as a recommendation to John, not as sent.
