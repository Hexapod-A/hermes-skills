# Outreach playbook (drafts for John to send)

## Cold email: 4 parts, under 120 words
1. **Specific opener** about *their* business (something observed on their site: "your site sends after-hours callers to voicemail").
2. **The cost of the gap** in plain words, no invented stats ("people who hit voicemail usually call the next clinic").
3. **What 3xAgentic does** in one sentence + the Pilot ("a 2-week pilot, one workflow, from $500").
4. **Soft ask**: "Worth a 15-minute call this week?" One link max (3xagentic.xyz). No attachments on first touch.

Subject lines: short, lowercase-ish, specific: "missed calls at {{business}}", "quick idea for {{business}}", "after-hours calls".

## Follow-ups
- Day 3: one line + a new angle (a booking or review idea).
- Day 7: a tiny proof: a 30-second Loom or a one-image mockup (made with flyer-studio), only if real.
- Day 14: break-up email ("should I close this out?").
Stop after 4 touches or at any "no". Never follow up with someone who opted out.

## DMs (Instagram / LinkedIn / Facebook)
- 2–3 sentences, no links in the first message, ask a question they can answer fast.
- Reference their own post or page. No copy-paste blasts.

## Compliance (US)
- CAN-SPAM: real sender name, John's business address in the footer, a clear opt-out line ("Reply 'no' and I won't email again"). Honour opt-outs.
- No texting or auto-dialling leads (TCPA). Calls are John's own, manual.
- No fake familiarity ("as we discussed"), no fake "Re:" subjects, no invented case studies.

## Voice
Casual, short, confident, human. 3xAgentic is an AI automation agency: never mention blockchain, crypto, tokens or Web3.
