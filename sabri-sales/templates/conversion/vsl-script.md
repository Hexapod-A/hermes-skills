# Conversion module: VSL (video sales letter) script

Sabri's rule from his "#1 business tip" short: use a video at every step. One video gets the call booked. One gets people to show up. One follows up with no-shows and non-buyers. One welcomes new clients.
His best service funnel: a VSL on the page, then a bridge video after booking.

John can record these on his phone: face to camera, or voice over a screen recording. No studio needed.

## Rules

- Open with the pain, not with "Hi, I'm John".
- Follow the 9 questions (see landing-page.md). The video must tell people exactly what to do next.
- Show the real workflow on screen when you explain "how it works".
- Real proof, real limit, the three guarantees. No invented results.
- Length: booking VSL 3-6 minutes. Bridge video 1-2 minutes. Short is fine if every line has a job.

## Booking VSL template

```text
[Hook]       {Pain question for the dream buyer}
[Agitate]    {What that task costs them: time, missed leads, tired staff. Their words, no made-up numbers.}
[Promise]    {What changes after the automation}
[Mechanism]  {How it works: audit -> plan -> build -> care. Show a real workflow.}
[New]        {Why this is different: task first, tool second; plain words; you keep the plan}
[Proof]      {3+ years, 23+ systems, 450+ hours, 12 industries}
[Risk]       {The three guarantees}
[Limit]      {Up to 10 audits a week, and why}
[CTA]        Book a free automation audit. {What happens on the call.}
```

## Filled example 1: booking VSL (home page)

```copy
[Hook] How many hours does your team spend each week typing the same thing twice?

[Agitate] For most businesses it is a lot. A lead form gets copied into the CRM. An invoice gets chased by email. A customer asks the same question your team answered yesterday. None of that grows the business. It just eats the week.

[Promise] Imagine those tasks done for you, every time, without anyone touching them.

[Mechanism] Here is how we do it at 3x Agentic. First, a free automation audit. On a short call we map how the work gets done today. Then you get a written plan: what to automate first, and what it will do. Then we build it and connect it to the tools you already use. Here is one we built. [Screen: a form entry arrives, the system adds it to the CRM and sends the reply.]

[New] We don't start with the shiny AI tool. We start with the boring task, because that is where the hours hide.

[Proof] We have done this for 3+ years. We have built 23+ systems and saved clients 450+ hours across 12 industries.

[Risk] You don't carry the risk. You pay 50% to start, and the other 50% only after you see it working. If you go ahead after a paid audit, the audit fee comes off the build. And after launch you get 6 months of fixes and upkeep.

[Limit] A builder does each audit, not a salesperson, so we do up to 10 audits a week.

[CTA] Click the button below and book a free automation audit. Bring one task that annoys you. We will show you what a system would do with it.
```

## Filled example 2: bridge video (after someone books)

```copy
You booked your free automation audit. Thank you.

Here is how to get the most from the call.

Before we talk, write down the tasks your team does by hand every day. The boring ones. The ones that get missed when you are busy.

On the call, we pick the task that costs you the most time. We show you what an automation would do with it.

You keep the plan, even if you build it yourself.

If the time no longer works, please move it with the link in your email. We only do up to 10 audits a week, and someone else can use the slot.

See you on the call.
```

## Filled example 3: follow-up video (after the audit, no decision yet)

```copy
Thanks again for the audit call.

The first automation handles {the task}. Your team stops doing it by hand.

A quick reminder on how we build. You pay 50% to start. You pay the other 50% only after you see it working. Then you get 6 months of fixes and upkeep.

If you have a question, reply to the email with this video. If you are ready, reply "go" and we will set the start date.
```
