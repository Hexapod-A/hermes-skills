# Conversion module: the automation audit call

Sabri: "The deal closes at discovery, not at the close." Diagnose like a doctor. Listen most of the call. Use their exact words.

## Call flow (agent prepares this sheet; John runs the call)

1. **Set the frame.** Say what will happen and how long. Say that at the end you will both decide yes or no. "Maybe" is worse than "no".
2. **Diagnose (most of the call).** Ask, then listen:
   - "Walk me through how {task} gets done today, step by step."
   - "Who does it? How often? What happens when they are busy or sick?"
   - "What have you tried already? What worked and what didn't?"
   - "If this task did itself, what would your team do with that time?"
   Write down their exact words. You will use them in the plan.
3. **Cost of doing nothing.** "What does it cost you if this stays the same for another year?" Let them say it. Don't invent a number for them.
4. **One feeds two (short demo).** In under 2 minutes, show a real workflow like theirs. Use their words to describe it.
5. **The plan.** Name the first automation and what it will do. Offer up to three options (small, medium, full). John sets the prices. The agent never invents them.
6. **Risk reversal.** The three guarantees: 50% to start and 50% after they see it working; audit fee credited to the build; 6 months of fixes and upkeep.
7. **Ask, then stay quiet.** "Does this make sense for you?" Then let them answer. Silence does the work.
8. **Objections: agree, then go back to the diagnosis.** Never argue. "That makes sense. You said earlier that {their words}. Is that still a problem?"
9. **Find the real objection.** "On a scale of 1 to 10, how ready are you?" Then: "What would make it a 10?"

## Follow-up after the call

Send within the day: the written plan, the options, the three guarantees, and the next step. Then follow up with value (see templates/outbound/follow-ups.md and the VSL follow-up video).
