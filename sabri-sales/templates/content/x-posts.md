# Content module: X posts and threads

Sabri's best X post was a list thread: "the business cheat codes I know now at 38, that I wish I knew at 28". Line 1 was his proof. Then one tip per tweet, each with a twist.
His one-liners that did well were short, quotable truths: "A strong offer will succeed in spite of a weak copy, but a strong copy won't overcome a weak offer."

## Rules

- Single posts: 1-5 short lines. One idea. No hashtags, or one at most.
- Threads: tweet 1 = hook + proof + promise. Then one lesson per tweet. Last tweet = CTA.
- Write for a business owner, not for other automation builders.
- Real proof only. No screenshots of income. No "I made $X".
- CTA in the last tweet or the reply, not in every tweet.

## Templates

```text
Single post:
{Contrarian or pain line}
{2-3 lines that make it concrete}
{Optional proof line}
{Optional soft CTA}

Thread:
1/ {What I learned building 23+ systems / saving clients 450+ hours} + {promise of the list}
2/ to N/ {One lesson each. One sentence + one example.}
Last/ If you want me to look at your business: book a free automation audit. 3xagentic.xyz
```

## Filled examples

### Example 1: single post, pain-first

```copy
Most small businesses don't need "AI".
They need the boring tasks to stop eating their week.
Copying data between apps. Chasing invoices. Answering the same questions all day.
We have saved clients 450+ hours by fixing only that.
```

```copy

4/ The best automation is invisible. Your team only notices that the task is gone.

5/ Don't buy a tool and hope. Map the task first. Then pick the tool.

6/ Pay for proof, not promises. We take 50% to start and the other 50% only after you see it working.

7/ If you want me to look at your business: book a free automation audit. 3xagentic.xyz
```
