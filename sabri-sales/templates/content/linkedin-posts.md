# Content module: LinkedIn posts

LinkedIn is where owners, operations managers and office managers read. Sabri's rules carry over: lead with pain, one idea per post, a story before the lesson, and always a next step.

## Rules

- Line 1 must earn the "see more" click. Pain, a surprising fact, or a short story start.
- Short paragraphs: 1-2 lines each. White space between them.
- 80-200 words. One lesson.
- No "I'm thrilled to announce". No hashtag walls (0-3 hashtags).
- End with a question to the reader or the CTA, not both.
- Real proof only. Never name a client without written OK.

## Template

```text
{Hook line: pain or story start}

{The situation in 2-3 short lines}

{What changed: the automation, in plain words}

{The lesson in one line}

{Proof line from the allowed list}

{CTA or a question}
```
