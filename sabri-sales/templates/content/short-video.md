# Content module: short videos (TikTok, Reels, YouTube Shorts)

What works for Sabri on short video (from his own numbers, Oct 2026 research):
- Reach comes from curiosity: a famous name, a "secret", a question people want answered.
- Shares and leads come from plain, useful tips told with an odd story first, lesson second.
- His best advice clip opens with a strange fact (a plane crash), then: "this follows my number one principle". One lesson. Then "take it and run".
- His bulk uploads of 7 clips in 2 days flopped. Post steadily, not in dumps.

For 3x Agentic: use the "lesson short" and the "teardown short". Skip luxury brands and hidden cameras.

## Rules

- 25-45 seconds. One idea per video.
- First 2 seconds: the hook on screen AND spoken. Pain or curiosity, never "Hi guys".
- Show, don't tell: screen-record the real workflow, or film the task by hand.
- One proof line from the allowed list. One CTA at the end.
- Captions on. Plain words. No jargon like "API" or "webhook" in the hook.
- CTA: "Book a free automation audit. Link in bio."

## Formats

1. **Lesson short**: odd fact or story → "this is the rule I build by" → the rule in one line → one proof line → CTA.
2. **Teardown short**: "I asked a {business type} how they handle {task}" → the manual steps on screen → the automated version on screen → CTA. Only with a real, consenting business, or a clearly labeled mock example.
3. **Myth short**: "Everyone says you need AI. You don't." → the boring task that matters → CTA.

## Template

```text
[0-2s, on screen + spoken] {Hook: pain or curiosity}
[2-10s] {The manual task, shown or described in one picture}
[10-25s] {The fix: what the automation does, step by step, in plain words}
[25-35s] {Rule or proof line}
[35-40s] Book a free automation audit. Link in bio.
```
