# Outbound module: follow-ups

Sabri: "Fortunes are made on follow-up." Most deals close after many touches, not one.
His method: map your real sales cycle, then plan more touches than feels normal. Each touch must add value or new information. Never "just checking in".

## Rules

- Each follow-up gives one new thing: a new idea, a short video, a question, or a useful fact about their business.
- Reply in the same email thread. Keep the subject.
- Short. 2-5 lines.
- Use the three guarantees in follow-ups. They answer the fear "what if I pay and it doesn't work?"
- Stop after the break-up message, or at any "no". Never follow up with someone who opted out.
- Suggested timing (internal, do not put in the copy): touch 2 after 3 days, touch 3 after 7 days, touch 4 after 14 days.

## Sequence template

```text
Touch 2 (new angle):   {A second task you noticed} + one line on what it costs + "Want to book a free automation audit?"
Touch 3 (proof/demo):  {A short Loom or one screenshot of a real workflow like theirs, only if it exists} + one line.
Touch 4 (risk):        The three guarantees in plain words + the real limit (10 audits a week).
Touch 5 (break-up):    "Should I close this out?" One line. No guilt.
```

## Filled examples (follow-ups to the dental clinic email)

### Touch 2: new angle

```copy
Hi Dr. Patel, one more idea.

Your reviews mention long hold times on the phone. Many of those calls are "can I move my appointment?"

An automation can handle reschedules by text and update your calendar, so the front desk answers fewer calls.

Want to book a free automation audit? We'll map both: the form and the calls.

John
```

### Touch 4: risk reversal

```copy
Hi Dr. Patel, owners often ask: "What if we pay for a system and it doesn't work?" Here is how we build:

You pay 50% to start. You pay the other 50% only after you see the system working in your clinic.
If you do a paid audit with us, the audit fee comes off the build price.
After launch, you get 6 months of fixes and upkeep. If something we built breaks, we fix it.

We do up to 10 audits a week. Want one of them?

John
```

### Touch 5: break-up

```copy
Hi Dr. Patel, I haven't heard back, so I'll guess the timing is wrong.

Should I close this out? If the booking form ever becomes a problem, reply here and I'll pick it up.

John
```
