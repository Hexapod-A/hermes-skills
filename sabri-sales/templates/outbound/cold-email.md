# Outbound module: cold email

Sabri ranks cold email as the number-two way to get clients, and the best one that needs no ad money.
His email rule: an email that looks personal gets opened. An email that looks like marketing does not.
"If somebody can smell from your subject line that your email is commercial, you're not going to get it opened."

## Rules

- One person, one business, one true detail you saw yourself (their site, their reviews, their job ads, their booking flow).
- Pain first. Name the manual task and what it costs them in plain words. No invented numbers.
- One offer: the free automation audit. One ask. No links in the first email except 3xagentic.xyz in the signature.
- Subject line: lower case, 2-5 words, looks like a note from a person. No emoji, no "Re:", no fake familiarity.
- Under 120 words. Short lines. No attachments.
- Use real proof only: 3+ years, 23+ systems, 450+ hours saved, 12 industries.
- Real scarcity only: "we do up to 10 audits a week". Never a deadline you made up.
- Footer: John's name, 3x Agentic, business address (CAN-SPAM), and an opt-out line.
- Drafts only. John sends from his own inbox.

## Template (pain → proof → offer → real limit → ask)

```text
Subject: {lower-case, specific: "your intake form" / "after-hours calls" / "invoice chasing"}

Hi {first name},

{One true detail you saw}. {The manual task this causes, in their words}.

{What that task costs them: time, missed leads, slow replies. No made-up numbers.}

We build automations that take that task off your team. In 3+ years we have built 23+ systems and saved clients 450+ hours.

Want to book a free automation audit? On a short call we map {the task} and show you what an automation would do. You keep the plan.

We do up to 10 audits a week, so I keep the list short.

John
3x Agentic, 3xagentic.xyz
{business address}
Reply "no" and I won't email again.
```
