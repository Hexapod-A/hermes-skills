# Outbound module: cold DMs (LinkedIn, X, Instagram)

Sabri's DM funnel: give value first, start a conversation, ask questions, then book the call.
A DM is a conversation, not a pitch. The first message gets a reply, not a sale.

## Rules for every platform

- 2-4 short lines. Write like a text to a person you respect.
- Open with something real about them: their post, their page, their job ad, their reviews.
- First message: no link and no pitch. Ask one question they can answer fast.
- Offer the free automation audit only after they reply, or in the second message.
- Real proof and real limits only. No "I help businesses scale". No "quick question" with no question.
- Never copy-paste the same DM to many people. Change the first line each time.
- Drafts only. John sends from his own accounts.

## Platform notes

- **LinkedIn**: best for owners, ops managers and office managers. Connect with a short note, or comment on a post first. Talk about their process, not "AI".
- **X**: reply to their post with a useful thought first. DM only if their DMs are open and you have a real reason.
- **Instagram**: best for local businesses (clinics, salons, gyms, trades). Reply to a story or a post. Talk about bookings, replies and missed messages.

## Template

```text
Message 1 (no pitch):
{Real detail from their post or page}. {One question about how they handle the task today}?

Message 2 (after they reply):
{Short answer that shows you understood}. We build automations for exactly that at 3x Agentic: 23+ systems so far, 450+ hours saved for clients.
If you want, book a free automation audit and we'll map it with you. We do up to 10 a week. {link only if they asked or said yes}
```

## Filled examples

### Example 1: LinkedIn, operations manager at a logistics company

```copy
Hi Dana, your post about "drowning in delivery confirmation emails" hit home.
Does your team copy those into a spreadsheet by hand, or does your system pull them in?
```

```copy
That makes sense. Copy-paste work like that is what we automate at 3x Agentic. We have built 23+ systems and saved clients 450+ hours.
If it helps, book a free automation audit and we'll map that inbox-to-spreadsheet step with you. We do up to 10 audits a week, so there is no rush, but the list is short.
```

### Example 2: X, a founder who posted about support tickets

```copy
Saw your post about answering the same support questions every day.
Which questions come up most? I'm curious if it's order status or setup help.
```

```copy
Order status is the easiest one to automate. A system can look up the order and reply with the real status in seconds.
We do this kind of build at 3x Agentic. Want to book a free automation audit? You keep the plan even if you build it yourself.
```

### Example 3: Instagram, a med spa with many comments asking about prices

```copy
Love the before-and-after posts. I noticed lots of comments ask "how do I book?"
Do you answer those by hand in the DMs right now?
```

```copy
Makes sense, that's many messages for a small team.
We build automations that answer booking questions in your DMs and send people to your calendar. Book a free automation audit and we'll show you how it would work for your page.
```
