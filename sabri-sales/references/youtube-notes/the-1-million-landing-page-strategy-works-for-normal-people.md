## Core idea (2-3 sentences)

A landing page converts based on *what* you say before *how* you say it — mine your market's actual language from reviews/forums instead of guessing or letting AI invent copy. Validate messaging angles cheaply on ad platforms before building the page, then assemble the page using a fixed structural sequence (headline → proof → offer → objections → urgency) matched to how cold or warm the traffic is.

## Frameworks and steps

1. **Mine verbatim voice-of-customer** — scrape Reddit, Google reviews, Trust Pilot, forums (your business + top 5 competitors); focus on 2-4 star reviews and the longest, most emotional posts. Feed raw text into AI and ask it to extract pains/fears/hopes/dreams and industry vernacular *verbatim*, not paraphrased.
2. **"Halo strategy"** — his worksheet template for categorizing all the scraped pains/fears/hopes/dreams before writing any copy.
3. **Dry testing** — turn your top ~10 hypothesized angles into separate headline+body ad variants, run them in one CBO on Meta for ~7 days, keep the 1-3 winners, run those another week to find the single best angle *before* building the full page.
4. **Match page to awareness level** (citing Eugene Schwartz's 5 stages) — label your traffic cold/warm/hot; build a separate page per stage; colder traffic needs more education/length, hot traffic needs less.
5. **Write like you talk** — record yourself pitching a cold stranger for 25-45 minutes (handle every objection A-to-Z), transcribe it, and use that transcript as the raw material for page copy instead of asking AI to write it cold.
6. **"17-step secret selling system"** (page skeleton, in order): call out audience (eyebrow) → big promise headline → sub-headline → build intrigue → lead-in (shine light on problem) → reveal solution → credentials → benefits not features → proof → Godfather offer → bonuses → build value/price anchor → reveal price → scarcity/urgency → guarantee → clear CTA → close with PS.
7. **Above-the-fold priority** — headline formula: "specific person + dream outcome + specific time frame + using [hero mechanism] + with decreased effort or a guarantee." Add a VSL above the fold; use an intriguing/weird thumbnail frame to boost play rate.
8. **Sub-headlines as hooks** — each should work as a stand-alone "hook" with an open loop; read only the headline + sub-headlines and they should tell the whole story.
9. **Proof must be louder than claims** — screenshots, before/afters, CRM/inbox proof for every claim made; remove any claim you can't prove.
10. **Hero mechanism (3 parts)** — (1) explain why other solutions fail, (2) explain why yours solves it, (3) name it with a proprietary-sounding term.
11. **Cold-traffic lead-gen offer** — use "food court test" (would a stranger shouted-at raise their hand?); offer a cheese offer (free value/education call, 30-60 min) not a whiskers offer ("book a call," "get a quote").
12. **Godfather offer** — hyper-specific outcome + timeframe, built-value pricing anchored high then revealed low, bold guarantee, 1-3 standalone-valuable bonuses.
13. **FAQ from buyers, not haters** — interview ~10 actual customers (what almost stopped you, what you tried before, what you'd tell a friend) and build the FAQ from themes, ordered biggest objection first.
14. **Social proof plugins/popups** — live "X just booked a call" notifications; must be real, adds roughly a further uplift on top of static proof.
15. **Page speed** — compress images (TinyPNG), delete unused plugins, minify code, avoid bloated CMS builders; test with Google PageSpeed/Pingdom, target under 1 second, prioritize mobile.
16. **Dial it in (ongoing split testing)** — test order of priority: angle → headline → sub-headline → lead-gen offer; track with third-party attribution (his examples: Wicked Reports, Hyros) and optimize on customer quality/LTV, not just conversion rate; let tests run 30+ days.

## Use for an AI automation agency

- Scrape real complaints from small-business owners (Reddit, FB groups, G2/Capterra reviews of competing tools, Google reviews of agencies) to find actual phrases about wasted time, missed leads, manual busywork — use those verbatim instead of guessing pain points.
- Before building a full landing page/VSL, test 3-5 angles (e.g., "stop losing leads to slow follow-up" vs. "save 10 hours/week on admin") as cheap ad copy or cold DM openers to see what gets replies.
- Segment messaging by awareness: cold LinkedIn/X audience needs education on *why* manual processes are costing them money before any pitch; warm outbound replies can go straight to a specific offer.
- Replace "book a call" CTAs with a genuinely valuable, specific offer — e.g., a free automation audit that identifies 2-3 concrete time-wasting steps in their business, delivered whether or not they buy.
- Build FAQ/objection-handling content (for the landing page, email sequences, DM scripts) from actual client interviews — what almost stopped them from booking, what tools they tried before.
- Only claim proof you can actually show — real screenshots of automations running, real time-saved numbers from real clients, not invented stats or fabricated urgency ("only 3 spots left" must be literally true if used at all).
- Name your automation methodology/process clearly (a "mechanism") so prospects understand why your build is different from a generic Zapier template — but only if it's an honest description of your actual approach.
- Keep pages/outreach fast, scannable, and benefit-led (what the business owner gets, not "we build AI agents") to respect how little attention cold prospects give before deciding to read further.

## Do not copy

- His scale claims (2,000 landing pages, 20M leads, $10.2B in sales, Shark Tank fame) are used as authority/credibility signals that a small agency cannot and should not fabricate or imply.
- Running paid Meta ad split-tests across 10 angles with real budget for a week+ assumes an ad budget a tiny agency likely doesn't have — the testing *logic* transfers, but cheap-channel equivalents (DM/email A/B subject lines, short video hooks) should replace paid ad validation.
- High-value "Godfather offer" anchoring with large build-value numbers (e.g., $50k-60k anchor prices) depends on an established agency's pricing power and sales team — don't invent fake "value" figures for automation builds.
- Dedicated video sales letters, custom scraping tools, paid attribution software (Hyros/Wicked Reports) and professional designers/developers assume budget and team resources a 3-person agency may not have; simplify accordingly rather than copying the full production stack.
- The urgency/scarcity tactic (stating caps on how many new clients you'll onboard) should only be used if literally true — don't manufacture fake capacity limits.
