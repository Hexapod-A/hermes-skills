## Core idea (2-3 sentences)

Meta's algorithm (Andromeda) is a creative-hungry matching engine: the more fresh, high-volume, native-feeling creative and long-form contextual copy you feed it, the better it finds buyers — so most of your effort should go into producing and testing variations, not into "ad-looking" polish or micromanaging targeting. Winning comes from volume + congruence (ad-to-landing-page match) + retargeting with new offers + tracking real cash metrics instead of vanity ROAS.

## Frameworks and steps

1. **Statics-over-video rule** — Prioritize static ad volume over slick video; produce many cheap static variations weekly rather than a few expensive videos, since Facebook's algorithm favors statics (can show more per session) [0:00-2:05].
2. **One Keyword Hack** — Take your winning ad, identify the niches that make up your top 20% of revenue clients, and duplicate the ad inserting that niche/keyword into headline or body copy as an "identity trigger" [2:05-4:09].
3. **Winning Format Clone** — Feed your winning ad into AI (Claude/ChatGPT/Kong), have it rewrite body copy, then headlines, then creative for different audience segments while keeping the same core message; load variants into a CBO and let Meta allocate spend [4:09-6:14].
4. **Zombie Campaign** — Collect ads you believe are good but got no spend in the CBO, bundle them into a new ad set, and relaunch to recover additional winners [6:14-7:17].
5. **Don't-look-like-an-ad rule** — Study content people already consume natively (your own organic-viral video, niche influencers via a "burner account") and run that as the ad creative instead of designing something ad-like [7:17-9:21].
6. **Broad Targeting + Specific Creative** — Stop interest-stacking; run broad targeting (country-level) with long-form, context-rich copy so the algorithm's targeting engine has enough signal to find the right people itself [9:21-10:24].
7. **Match Ad to Landing Page** — Test many headlines inside Meta ads (cheap, high-volume data), find the best performer, and mirror it in the landing page headline/subhead/lead-in for congruence; always run at least 3 split tests at once [10:24-12:29].
8. **Retarget With a Different Offer** — If someone doesn't buy, don't just re-serve the same ad; retarget with a different product/offer, objection-handling ads, testimonial/proof ads, and a value-first audit offer [12:29-15:37].
9. **Track Blended ROAS / Net Cash, Not Ad-Level ROAS** — Find your real breakeven cost-to-acquire-customer and scale spend toward that ceiling even if ROAS % drops, because total profit matters more than the ratio; review raw numbers yourself monthly [15:37-17:42].

## Use for an AI automation agency

- Make many cheap static ad/creative variants (problem/benefit headlines about automation pain points) weekly instead of investing all effort in one polished video — test volume, not perfection.
- Apply the "one keyword hack" honestly: segment by real vertical (e.g., "automation for HVAC companies," "automation for dental clinics") based on which niches actually convert for you, not invented ones.
- Don't make cold DMs/cold emails or short videos look like ads — mirror the native tone of LinkedIn/X posts and real founder content in your niche rather than salesy copy.
- Ensure your landing page/VSL headline matches whatever hook got the click in your cold email, DM, or post — congruence reduces bounce and builds trust.
- When a lead doesn't book a call, retarget or follow up with a different angle (e.g., a different automation use case or a free audit) rather than repeating the same pitch.
- Track real outcomes (booked calls → paying builds → net profit) rather than vanity metrics like open rates or impressions when deciding where to spend time/money.
- Use a free, genuinely useful "audit" style offer (e.g., a short automation opportunity report) as a value-first lead magnet, as long as it's real value delivered honestly, not a bait-and-switch.
- Keep testing: run at least a couple of message/headline variants at any time in outbound and landing pages, and let real reply/conversion data — not assumptions — decide what to keep.
