# Voice and plain-English rules (John's standing rules)

## Plain, simplified English (ASD-STE100 style) for all public copy
The reader is a busy, non-technical business owner reading on a phone.
- One idea per sentence. Aim for 15 words or fewer; 20 is the limit (the lint warns above 20).
- Active voice: "We build the system", not "The system is built".
- Common words: use, help, start, need, get, show. Not utilize, leverage, facilitate, seamless, synergy.
- One word, one meaning. Say "automation" or "system" and keep it. Don't switch to "solution", "workflow engine", "agentic stack".
- No tech words in hooks or headlines: no API, webhook, LLM, agentic, n8n, Zapier (unless the reader used the word first).
- Concrete over clever: "replies to every booking request at once" beats "streamlines engagement".
- Short paragraphs: 1-3 lines.

## Sabri's voice, kept and dropped
Keep: short lines, pain first, one big benefit + curiosity, a story before the lesson, a clear next step every time, confidence.
Drop: swearing, slang ("murdering money"), "Internet Daddy" jokes, money flexing, luxury brands, mock-legal jokes, hype adjectives.

## Banned (the lint fails the draft)
- Words: hustle, grind, passive income, financial freedom, game-changer, "Automation Mastery".
- Any blockchain, crypto, Web3, NFT or token-sale language. 3x Agentic is AI only.
- Gemini (do not mention it).
- Prices, price ranges, "$", "per month". Pricing is not in this skill.
- Fake urgency: "act now", "limited time", "last chance", "hurry", "ends soon", "spots filling fast".
- "Free build", "money back", "refund".
- Any number outside the allowed list (see `offer-stack.md`).

## Fixed words
- Wordmark: **3x Agentic** (lower-case x, one space). Never 3xAgentic, 3X Agentic. The domain stays 3xagentic.xyz.
- CTA: **Book a free automation audit**. Same words every time. Link: 3xagentic.xyz.
- Market: USD clients (US first). US spelling.
- Sign-off for outbound: "John" then "3x Agentic, 3xagentic.xyz".

## Honesty rules
- Every claim about a prospect must be something seen on their site, reviews, ads or posts. Note where in the draft file.
- No invented stats, results, testimonials, client names or "trusted by".
- No fake familiarity ("as we discussed"), no fake "Re:" subjects.
- A mock example is labeled "mock example".
- If you don't know a fact, leave a {placeholder} and ask John.
