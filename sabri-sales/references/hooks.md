# Hooks (attention)

Each hook type: Sabri's real example (with where it ran and its numbers, from the Oct 18, 2026 research), then 3x Agentic versions that pass the lint.
Rule: a hook needs one clear benefit or pain AND a reason to keep reading. Test several hooks for one piece; Sabri's rule is "don't make ads, make campaigns with lots of hooks".

## 1. Pattern break: odd story first, lesson second
Sabri: "If you accidentally fall out of a plane, most people think you should aim to land in water…" then "This follows my number one principle… Don't make your ads look like ads." (TikTok, 121.7K views, 1,008 shares, his best share rate.)
3x Agentic:
- "Your team is doing a robot's job."
- "I asked an owner what his new hire would do all day. Each task on the list was a job for a system."

## 2. Time-boxed or "fast course"
Sabri: "Give Me 27 Minutes and I'll Make You Disgustingly Good at Digital Marketing" (357K) · "Learn 97% of Sales in 52 Minutes" (212K).
3x Agentic (no invented numbers; use real lengths only once the video exists):
- "Everything I learned building 23+ automation systems, in one video."
- "Watch this before you pay anyone for AI."

## 3. Proof number in the first line
Sabri: "$300m of Meta Ads Hacks in Under 22 mins" (167K).
3x Agentic (allowed numbers only):
- "We have saved clients 450+ hours. Here is the boring task behind most of them."
- "23+ systems built across 12 industries. These mistakes show up in every one."

## 4. Contrarian
Sabri: "My #1 business tip for this year isn't AI" (YT Short, 42K; the tip is "use a VSL at every step").
3x Agentic:
- "Most small businesses don't need AI. They need the boring tasks gone."
- "Don't hire another assistant until you read this."

## 5. List / "what I wish I knew"
Sabri: "Here are the business cheat codes I know now at 38, that I wish I knew back when I was 28" (X, 348 likes, 28.7K views).
3x Agentic:
- "What I wish every owner knew before they paid for 'AI':"
- "Signs your business is ready for automation:"

## 6. Ranking / tier list
Sabri: "Blind ranking lead generation" (TikTok, 20.9K) · "I Ranked the Best Client Getting Funnels (2026)" (35K).
3x Agentic:
- "I ranked the tasks small businesses should automate first."
- "Chatbot, booking, invoices, lead follow-up: which one to automate first?"

## 7. Exposé / teardown (use with care)
Sabri: "Apparently it's impossible to buy a Birkin bag, let's put it to the test." (TikTok, 5.0M) · "How Louis Vuitton Brainwashes You…" (135K).
3x Agentic (real, consenting business or a clearly labeled mock only):
- "How a clinic loses bookings at night (mock example)."
- "I filled in {N} contact forms at night. Here is what happened." (Only if John really did it; use the real count.)

## Subject lines (cold email)
Lower case, 2-5 words, about THEIR business: "your intake form" · "after-hours calls" · "quotes at {company}" · "invoice chasing" · "zillow leads".
Never: "Re:", "quick question" with no question, emoji, ALL CAPS, "partnership opportunity".

## Hook checklist
- Does it name a pain or a benefit the dream buyer cares about?
- Is there a reason to read line 2?
- Is every number on the allowed list?
- Would a busy owner understand it with no tech words?
