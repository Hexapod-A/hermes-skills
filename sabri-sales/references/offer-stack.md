# The 3x Agentic offer stack

Confirmed by John on Oct 24, 2026. Use these words. Do not add terms, prices or promises.

## The ladder

1. **Free automation audit** (the lead magnet / HVCO). A short call with a builder. We find the tasks that eat the team's time and show what an automation would do. The owner keeps the plan.
   CTA everywhere: **Book a free automation audit** (3xagentic.xyz).
2. **Paid audit** (see "Open point" below). A deeper look that produces the full build plan. Its fee is credited to the build.
3. **The build.** We build the system and connect it to the tools the client already uses. Paid 50/50 (below).
4. **Care.** 6 months of fixes and upkeep after launch.
5. **The next build.** Every plan names "what to automate next". (Sabri: engineer the second sale.)

## The three guarantees (risk reversal)

Use the exact meaning. Short versions are fine; never widen them.

| Name | Public wording | Never say |
|---|---|---|
| Milestone pay | "You pay 50% to start. You pay the other 50% only after you see the system working in your business." | "pay nothing until…", "free build", "money back" |
| Audit credit | "If you go ahead with the build, the audit fee comes off the build price." | that the free audit has a fee; any fee amount |
| 6 months of care | "6 months of fixes and upkeep after launch. If something we built breaks, we fix it. Fixes and upkeep, not new features." | "unlimited changes", "free updates", "free new features", "support forever" |

One-line version: "Pay half after you see it work. Audit fee credited. 6 months of fixes and upkeep."

Damaging admission that makes them believable (Sabri's premium-price method): "We don't promise a number of hours saved before we see your process. We do promise you only pay the second half once it works, and we fix what we built for 6 months."

## Proof (the only numbers allowed)

- 3+ years building automations
- 23+ systems built
- 450+ hours saved for clients
- Clients in 12 industries
- Up to 10 automation audits a week (capacity)
Plus the terms above: 50%, 6 months. Nothing else. No client names, logos or quotes unless John supplies them in writing.

## Real scarcity

"We do up to 10 audits a week, because a builder does each one, not a salesperson."
Allowed: say the weekly limit and the reason. Say "this week's audits are full" only when John says so.
Not allowed: countdowns, "only 2 spots left" (unless John gives that exact live number), "price goes up Friday", "offer ends".

## The Godfather test (run before publishing any offer copy)

Score each line yes/no. Fix every "no".
1. Is the dream outcome stated in the buyer's words (time back, leads answered, invoices paid)?
2. Is it clear what they get from the free audit (a plan they keep)?
3. Is the risk on us, not them (the three guarantees)?
4. Is the reason for the limit real and stated?
5. Is there exactly one next step?
6. Are all numbers on the allowed list, and is there no price?
7. Would John be comfortable if the client read it back to him in a dispute?

## Open point for John

This skill reads that as two steps: a free first audit call, then an optional paid audit (deeper plan) whose fee is credited to the build. Until John confirms, copy says "If you go ahead after a paid audit, the audit fee comes off the build" and never states the fee.
