# Design rules (the 3xAgentic look)

Distilled from John's own sample posts and the flyers built for him in code.

**Canvas.** Pure black (`#000000`). Off-white text (`#F1F1F1`), never pure white for long copy. One accent, orange `#FF9900`.

**Type.** Headlines in DM Sans, regular weight (400), tight tracking (-0.025em), line-height ~1.05, 2 lines, Title Case, ~100px on a 1080 canvas. Body in Plus Jakarta Sans 34px, line-height 1.4, max ~60 characters per line. Accent at most one or two words per headline (`*Money*`).

**Logo.** Top centre (centred layouts) or top left (left layouts), ~64px tall, generous clear space (≥ 28px) below.

**Decor.** Chunky pixel blocks on a 104px grid in orange with small off-white squares, cropped by the canvas edge: bottom-right cluster for centred layouts, a right-edge column for left-aligned layouts. A small off-white checker stair-step in the bottom-left corner. A thin off-white ↖ arrow in a black notch of the blocks. Decor never touches text.

**CTA.** `3xagentic.xyz` in an off-white pill with an orange starburst tucked over its left end, or as plain text in left-aligned layouts.

**Rhythm.** Big margins (96px sides, ~104px top). Content block vertically centred in the space between logo and decor. Nothing within 24px of an edge. Even gaps (32–52px) between blocks.

**Series.** Alternate centred and left-aligned layouts across a set so a grid of posts doesn't look copy-pasted. Keep the same CTA everywhere.

**Copy.** Clean grammar, no hype words, outcome-first (missed calls, booked appointments, hours saved). 3xAgentic is an AI automation agency: never crypto, blockchain, Web3 or token wording. Claims need a source the user gave or one you cited; otherwise cut the claim.

**Do not:** use gradients, glows, stock 3D icons, emoji as graphics, drop shadows on text, more than two type sizes per block, or any AI-generated imagery.
