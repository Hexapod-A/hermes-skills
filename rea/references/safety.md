# Scope and safety

The skill teaches measurement and reconstruction, not intrusion. Work on your own systems, systems
you are allowed to test, or public interfaces you may lawfully observe. Keep requests within
published limits. If a page is blocked, `web-unblock` is a read-only fallback for public content,
not a reason to defeat a login. Do not run credential stuffing, exploit code, invasive tracing on
third-party devices, or copy another person's private data. Use test accounts with permission.

Never put tokens, cookies, card details, personal data or private HAR bodies into committed
artifacts. Redact a copy and keep the raw file in a secure local location when it is needed. Do not
publish someone else's paid assets in the replica. State what you could not access, rather than
writing a guessed result as observed.
